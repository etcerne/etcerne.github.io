A Statistical Exploration of Negro Leagues Baseball ↗

This project comprises 4 explorations of Negro Leagues statistics, which are compiled from the Seamheads Negro Leagues Database.

Focus Areas: Sabermetrics, Historical Baseball Data, Statistical Analysis
